What each status means
Investigating
The Agent's Run is waiting for a worker or running. An earlier report stays on the incident page while it works.
Root cause found
A report is published and no recovery watch is running.
Watching recovery
The Observer (code, no model) samples health after a published report or a person marked it remediated.
Needs attention
The Agent stopped without a report, the Run was canceled, or no single target matched the alert.
Paused
Paused by a person or a suspension; no investigation until resumed.
Human-owned
A person took over; the Agent does not investigate.
Resolved
The recovery watch confirmed recovery and nobody closed it yet.
Closed / Merged
Closed by a person, or folded into another incident. One whose postmortem waits for review also appears under Needs attention.

The label under a status says the usual next step; the incident page has the actions.
